# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small private groups of people who already share a life together: couples, friend groups and families, weighted equally. They arrive on the web, often from a link a friend sent ("rejoins notre bande"), or after hearing about the app. On the public site they are curious, a bit skeptical, and want to understand in under a minute what Bobine does and whether it is worth creating an account. Inside the app (product surfaces) they are on their phone, right after a night out, a dinner or a trip, adding photos and writing their impressions.

## Product Purpose

Bobine turns a group's shared moments into a series they can relive. A group is a "bande" in the interface (a "space" in the code and database); each outing, dinner or trip is an "episode"; the period the group lives through together is a "season". Members propose and vote on ideas for the next outing, and each member writes a private review of an episode that stays hidden until everyone has written theirs, then all reviews are revealed at once.

The public landing page exists to explain this in plain words and convert visitors into accounts (sign up) or send returning members to sign in. Success: a visitor understands the bande / episode / season idea and the secret-review reveal without reading a manual, and clicks "Créer un compte".

## Brand Personality

Complice, festif, spontané. The energy of the group chat right after a great night: laughter, inside jokes, "on se refait ça quand ?". French, informal "tu", short sentences, a little humor, never cheesy. The series metaphor (episodes, seasons, the reveal) is used with a wink, not as a lecture. Emotional goals: warmth, belonging, a small thrill of anticipation (the reveal).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS startup pages: icon-grid feature cards, purple gradients, giant vanity metrics ("10 000 utilisateurs").
- Childish: candy colors, mascots, emojis everywhere.
- Dating apps (Tinder, Bumble aesthetics): Bobine is about groups and shared memories, not seduction.
- Public social networks: no likes, followers or infinite feeds. Bobine is private, between people who know each other.

## Design Principles

1. **Show people, not features.** Real faces, real outings, real laughter carry the message before any copy does.
2. **Explain the metaphor once, then play with it.** Space, episode, season and the reveal are defined in ordinary words the first time they appear.
3. **Private by default, and say so.** Reassure that only invited people see a bande, without sounding like a security product.
4. **One clear next step per fold.** Create an account is always reachable; sign in is always one tap away for returning members.
5. **Warm daylight, cinematic payoff.** Sunny, human openings; the "series" moments (reveal, season recap) can borrow the app's darker cinema world.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, visible focus, keyboard-reachable CTAs, meaningful alt text on every photo. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (no entrance choreography when reduced). Plain language readable by every generation in a family. Diverse people in imagery (ages, origins, group types).

## Brand

The product is called **Bobine** (formerly Gather). Its identity system (logo, palette, type, voice, components) lives in the "Bobine · Système d'identité" design system. In short: the logo is a film reel that also reads as a round table seen from above, five places around a shared moment (the yellow hub). Palette: red #D7372A for brand and the main action, sun #F5C542 for the shared moment and reveals, warm paper #FBF6EE by day, cinema black #151012 at night. Type: Bricolage Grotesque for titles, Figtree for text, Gochi Hand only for revealed reviews and captions.
